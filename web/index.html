Life questions, answered from the Bhagavad Gita & Ashtavakra Gita.

Bhagavad Gita

A 700-verse dialogue between prince Arjuna and Krishna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra (part of the epic Mahabharata). Its heart is selfless action — doing your duty without clinging to the results.

Ashtavakra Gita

A short, piercing dialogue between the sage Ashtavakra and King Janaka on non-dual awareness (Advaita). Its message is radical and direct: you are already free — pure awareness, never bound.

Both are classical Sanskrit wisdom texts from the Indian contemplative tradition. Pick one to focus the answer, or “Both” to compare.
